# Solo Spot

Paste a YouTube link to any song. Solo Spot finds a natural place for a guitar solo (usually after the second chorus), keeps the band playing for the number of bars you pick, then brings the vocals back in. It also tells you the key and which scale to use.

Open it at **https://eminicu.github.io/solo-spot/**

## How it works
- **Where the vocals are:** time-synced lyrics from LRCLIB show when singing starts and stops.
- **Key and tempo:** AcousticBrainz audio analysis of the recording, plus 30-second Apple Music and Deezer previews analyzed on your phone.
- **The solo:** at the solo point the player jumps back by a whole number of bars to a stretch with no singing, so the groove carries on. The app learns how long jumps take on your device and keeps the beat lined up.
