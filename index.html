Take the solo

  1. In YouTube, tap Share → Copy link, then tap Paste here.
  2. Tap the video to start. Solo Spot finds the key and builds a backing track: the song's chorus with the vocals removed.
  3. When the light turns red, it's yours. Right before the last chorus the band plays the chorus without vocals for the bars you picked, then the real chorus comes in. Or pick the ending solo and jam as long as you like.

Use the "official audio" upload of a song when you can: its timing matches the data Solo Spot uses.
